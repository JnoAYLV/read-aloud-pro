# House style (built-in, edit to suit)

These are the writing rules this team uses. To change them, open Settings → Claude & Library,
click "Make editable copy" on this guide, edit the copy, and the original is switched off.

- Do not use em dashes. Use a full stop, a comma or a colon instead.
- Say "gateways", not "sites", for our operating locations.
- Never name a person as the cause of a failure. Describe the process, the shift or the role.
- Use plain words. Prefer "use" to "utilize", "help" to "facilitate", "start" to "commence".
- Dates: "7 Nov" or "Fri 7 Nov". Times: 24-hour local time with the station, e.g. "04:10 CT".
- Gateway codes in capitals as written in the source (e.g. KMSP).
- Percentages to one decimal place unless the source uses another precision.

## Company terms
