Anywhere on your PC: highlight text, then Ctrl+Alt+R to read · Ctrl+Alt+M add to memory · Ctrl+Alt+O read screen · Ctrl+Alt+V talk

Welcome to Read Aloud Pro

Highlight text in any program, such as an Outlook email, a Word document, a PDF or a web page, and press Ctrl+Alt+R. It appears here and is read aloud with each word highlighted.

Press Ctrl+Alt+O to read the text on your screen, even where it can't be highlighted. Use Open file for Word documents and PDFs.

The panel on the right has the voice, speed and speaker, the Find tab for your highlight words, and the Claude tab for memory, summaries, emails and documents.
